### Existing Shop Product Inquiry

For existing 3D Figs products, please visit the shop at https://3dfigs.com/shop. If you have a question about a specific product, please provide the product name or link, the quantity you are interested in, and any customization request.

### Custom 3D Print Inquiry

3D Figs may be a good fit for custom 3D printed products, gifts, collectibles, figurines, and small-batch 3D printed items. To evaluate the request, please provide the type of item, desired size, quantity, color preferences, deadline, and whether you already have a 3D model file.

For 3D printing from a model file, please provide the file type, such as STL, 3MF, GLB, or another 3D format, along with the desired size, color, quantity, and intended use. A file review may be needed before confirming printability, pricing, or production timing.

### Traditional Printing Inquiry That Should Be Routed Elsewhere
