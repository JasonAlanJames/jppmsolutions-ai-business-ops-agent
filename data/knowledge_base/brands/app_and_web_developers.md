# App & Web Developers

## Overview

App & Web Developers provides website services, domain services, website hosting, app development, web application development, and custom agentic AI system development for businesses. This brand is the technology services arm connected to JPPM Solutions and should be used for customers who need technical implementation, hosting, automation, AI agents, enterprise AI assistants, websites, apps, or web-based business systems.

App & Web Developers is separate from AI Agent Innovation Academy. AI Agent Innovation Academy focuses on AI certification courses and training, while App & Web Developers focuses on building real-world technology solutions for clients. When a customer wants something built, automated, hosted, integrated, designed, or deployed, App & Web Developers is usually the correct routing destination.

## Reply Guidance

When App & Web Developers is the best fit, the agent should respond in a professional, consultative, business-focused tone. The reply should make it clear that App & Web Developers can help with technology strategy, website development, app development, hosting, domains, AI agents, AI automation, and custom enterprise systems.

The agent should include the correct website based on the customer’s need:

- For general app, website, and AI development inquiries: https://appandwebdevelopers.com
- For website-specific service inquiries: https://websites.appandwebdevelopers.com
- For hosting-specific inquiries: https://hosting.appandwebdevelopers.com

The agent should provide this contact email when appropriate:

- info@appandwebdevelopers.com

The agent should ask clarifying questions when project details are missing. For example, the agent may ask about the type of website or app needed, existing domain, hosting needs, desired features, timeline, budget range, required integrations, AI automation goals, and whether human approval should be included in AI workflows.

The agent should not invent prices, timelines, hosting guarantees, development guarantees, support guarantees, security guarantees, or technology commitments unless those details exist in the approved knowledge base.

If a customer asks for a quote, the agent should gather the necessary details and draft a reply for human approval. It should not send the email automatically.

## Lead Qualification Questions

Use these questions when a customer asks about website development:

- What type of website do you need?
- Do you already have a domain name?
- Do you need website hosting?
- Do you need a new website or a redesign?
- What pages do you need?
- What features should the website include?
- Do you need ecommerce, booking, forms, memberships, courses, or customer portals?
- Do you have branding, logo files, images, and written content ready?
- What is your desired launch timeline?
- Do you have a budget range?

Use these questions when a customer asks about app development:

- Do you need an iOS app, Android app, web app, or cross-platform app?
- What problem should the app solve?
- Who are the users?
- What are the main features?
- Do users need accounts or logins?
- Does the app need payments, subscriptions, notifications, maps, media uploads, chat, or admin dashboards?
- Does the app need to connect to an existing website, API, CRM, database, or third-party platform?
- What is your target launch timeline?
- Do you have a budget range?

Use these questions when a customer asks about AI agent or automation development:

- What business process do you want to automate?
- Who will use the AI system?
- Will the AI answer customers, assist staff, or both?
- What knowledge base or documents should the AI use?
- Does the AI need access to email, CRM, forms, website chat, support tickets, or databases?
- Should the AI classify, summarize, draft replies, route leads, answer questions, or take actions?
- Which actions require human approval?
- Should the AI be allowed to send emails, update records, or trigger workflows only after approval?
- What security or privacy rules must the system follow?
- What would a successful result look like?

## Safety and Accuracy Rules

- Do not invent pricing.
- Do not invent project timelines.
- Do not promise same-day delivery unless approved.
- Do not promise guaranteed search engine rankings.
- Do not promise guaranteed app store approval.
- Do not promise unlimited hosting resources unless approved.
- Do not provide legal, tax, or compliance advice.
- Do not expose customer data.
- Do not expose API keys, tokens, credentials, private server details, or internal infrastructure.
- Do not claim a system can perform an external action unless human approval rules allow it.
- Do not send emails automatically.
- Draft replies only and wait for human approval before sending.
