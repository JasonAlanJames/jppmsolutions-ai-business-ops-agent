# Tripping AI

## Overview

Tripping AI is an agentic AI travel assistant and travel-planning brand connected to JPPM Solutions. The platform is designed to help users ask travel-related questions, explore trip ideas, plan destinations, and interact with an AI travel agent through a dedicated chat interface.

Tripping AI should be selected when customers ask about AI-powered travel planning, destination ideas, itinerary help, travel questions, the Tripping AI website, or the Tripping AI chat interface. Tripping AI is different from AI Agent Innovation Academy, which focuses on AI education and certification courses, and App & Web Developers, which focuses on custom AI system development and technical services.
